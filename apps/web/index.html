Our promises

What ShambaSense will never do

Farmers lose money when an app sounds more sure than it is. These rules are built into the product.

  • Show a 94% sure

    We use plain words like Likely and Not sure, and the app is allowed to say it doesn't know.

  • Sell you a spray

    ShambaSense never recommends a pesticide product or dose. That decision belongs to a qualified agronomist.

  • Say synced too early

    Nothing shows as sent until our server has it. Waiting records are always counted on screen.

  • Guess offline

    A check taken with no signal waits in the queue. It never gets a made-up answer.
