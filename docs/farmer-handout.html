  1. 1

    Fungua akaunti

    Fungua ShambaSense, chagua Kiswahili, kisha weka nambari yako ya simu. Utapokea SMS yenye nambari ya tarakimu 6. Usimpe mtu nambari hii.

    Create your account

    Open ShambaSense, choose your language and enter your phone number. An SMS brings a 6-digit code. Never share this code.

  2. 2

    Ongeza vipande vyako

    Kwenye Shamba, ongeza kila kipande: jina lake, zao, na tarehe ya kupanda.

    Add your plots

    In Shamba, add each field: its name, the crop, and the planting date.

  3. 3

    Angalia zao lenye ugonjwa

    Bonyeza kitufe cha kamera Angalia. Picha nzuri:

    • Karibu na jani moja au tunda moja
    • Mwanga wa mchana, bila flash
    • Shika simu bila kutikisika
    Inawezekana zaidiInawezekanaPiga picha nyingine

    Check a sick crop

    Tap the camera button, Check. A good photo is:

    • Close to one leaf or fruit
    • In daylight, without the flash
    • Held still until it is sharp
    More likelyPossibleNeed another photo
  4. 4

    Uliza mtaalamu

    Kama jibu halina uhakika, bonyeza Uliza mtaalamu. Mtaalamu wa chama atajibu kwenye simu yako. Akiomba picha ya pili, piga picha ya mmea ule ule baada ya siku chache.

    Ask an agronomist

    If the answer is not sure, tap Ask an agronomist. Your co-op's agronomist replies in the app. If they ask for a follow-up photo, photograph the same plant again a few days later.

  5. 5

    Andika kazi ya kila siku

    Kwenye Daftari, andika kazi, pembejeo, mavuno na mauzo. Nyumbani utaona faida na matumizi ya wiki 8.

    Write down each day's work

    In Journal, record work, inputs, harvest and sales. Home shows money in and out over 8 weeks.

  6. 6

    Jiunge na chama chako

    Kwenye Wasifu, bonyeza Jiunge na chama na uweke nambari ya chama iliyo juu ya karatasi hii. Unaweza kuondoka wakati wowote.

    Join your co-op

    In Profile, tap Join a co-op and type the co-op code at the top of this sheet. You can leave at any time.

Hakuna mtandao? Endelea tu. Kila kitu kinahifadhiwa kwenye simu na kitatumwa chenyewe ukipata mtandao. Hali ya hewa ya siku 3 iko Nyumbani.

No signal? Keep going. Everything is saved on the phone and sends by itself when there is signal. Home shows a 3-day weather forecast.

Kabla ya kunyunyiza dawa, soma lebo na umuulize mtaalamu wa chama. Before spraying any chemical, read the label and ask your co-op agronomist.
